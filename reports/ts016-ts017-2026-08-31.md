## Numerical and contract evidence

`tests/bed-placement.test.ts` covers module decomposition (including unbuildable 7 AL), coordinate transforms, overlap/clearance, support containment, connected-bed validation, three-/four-point baseline identity, round trips and export guards. Independent PPUs are checked for mass/COG conservation, hydraulic totals, host-beam load station, wind/inertia and road-force effects. Invalid, unsecured, off-deck and cargo-intersecting PPUs are rejected. Unequal manual trains each receive support/beam assessment, including a deliberately failing short train.

The deterministic angled-search regression evaluated 17 retained candidates: 17 passed, including 12 angled candidates across both hydraulic modes. Straight candidates remained present. Reapplying candidates reproduced the exact engineering results. This proves the tested bounded families work, not global optimality.

The two CAD fixtures contain three beds (two joined 6-AL beds at +10°, one independent 5-AL bed at −5°), an attached PPU and a secured independent PPU. Each totals 17 AL, 198.9 t and 12.4 t of PPU mass. Their deliberately unsupported short-train setup remains labelled SUPPORT_FAIL. Assertions verify three/four authoritative boundary points, both signed bed rotations, 16 PPU drawing entities across the two fixtures, PPU attributes and G1/G4 pressure values. Five malformed cases cover wrong version, wrong units, missing views, invalid coordinates and unsecured PPU. No imported text is evaluated as LISP.
